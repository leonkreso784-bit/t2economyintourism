Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 16 September 2026

What is Sokrat Study?

A free study platform with two ways in: study a ready subject from the catalogue, or build your own material from your notes. Either way it works the same — read it, then practise it with flashcards, a quiz and fill-in-the-blanks, and your progress is saved.

Is it free?

Yes. Studying and building your own material are free, and there are no ads.

Which subjects are in the catalogue?

University subjects from the Faculty of Tourism and Hospitality Management in Opatija (FMTU): the Hospitality Management programme in English, and first-year subjects in Croatian. Some subjects also have practice exercises or a blind map. Your subject isn't there? Build it yourself — it works the same way.

Do I need an account?

Not to study the catalogue — without one, your progress is saved in this browser. A free account lets you build your own material, keeps your progress in sync across devices, and gives you a profile. Sign up with email and password, or continue with Google. You need to be at least 16.

How does syncing work?

When you're signed in, your progress is backed up while you study, and your materials, profile and colour theme follow you to every device you sign in on. Progress from different devices is merged, so nothing you've learned is lost.

How do I make my own material?

Sign in and open My materials. Use + New to create a folder or a material. In a material you write the explanation and add flashcards, quiz questions and fill-in-the-blanks — images too. Tap it to study, with the same screens as the catalogue. Building is easiest on a computer; studying works everywhere.

Who can see my material and my profile?

Only you. Your materials are private, and so is your profile for now. One exception: your profile photo and cover image can be opened by anyone who has the image's direct link — see the Privacy Policy.

Can I study offline?

Yes. Open a subject from the catalogue and choose Download for offline — it then works without a connection.

Can I switch the language?

Yes — the language button at the top switches the interface between English and Croatian. Study material stays in the language it was written in.

Will you send me emails?

Only if you said yes — when you signed up, or under Profile → Email updates. We write now and then about a new subject or an important change, never ads, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Emails you ask for, like confirming your address or resetting your password, come either way.

How do I delete my data or my account?

In the app, under Profile → Privacy & data. Delete study history erases your progress from the cloud and this device and keeps your account. Delete account permanently removes everything — progress, profile, photos, materials and their images. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Found a mistake, or something isn't working?

Write to sokrat@sokratstudy.com. For a mistake, tell us the subject and lesson; for a problem, what you did and on which device.
