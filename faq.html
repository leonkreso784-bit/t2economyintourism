Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 12 June 2026

What is Sokrat Study?

A free interactive study platform for university students. Each subject offers in-depth lessons, flashcards, quizzes, fill-in exercises, and progress tracking — built specifically for exam preparation.

Is it really free?

Yes — all subjects and all study modes are completely free.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing up — your progress is saved in your browser. A free account (email + password) adds cloud backup of your progress and lets you continue seamlessly on another device.

How does progress sync work?

When you are signed in, your progress (learned flashcards, quiz scores, statistics) is automatically backed up to the cloud while you study. Signing in on another device merges your progress — nothing you've learned is ever lost.

Which subjects are available?

Subjects from the Faculty of Tourism and Hospitality Management (Opatija), Hospitality Management programme — including Marketing, Tourism Economics, Accounting, Tourism Geography, Food & Nutrition, Economics in Hospitality, and more. New subjects are added regularly.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. In the app, open Profile → Privacy & data → Delete cloud data. For full account deletion, email leonkreso784@gmail.com. Details in our Privacy Policy.

I found a mistake in the content — what should I do?

Please tell us! Email leonkreso784@gmail.com with the subject, lesson, and the issue, and we'll fix it.

Can I contribute my own materials?

Absolutely — students are encouraged to send notes, summaries, or exam scripts to leonkreso784@gmail.com.
