The four ways

Every subject — from the catalog or your own — opens in all four. Nothing to set up.

  • Learn

    The material itself — sections, images, tables, formulas.

  • Flashcards

    Question on one side, answer on the other. Short by design.

  • Quiz

    Multiple choice, scored, with the reason behind each answer.

  • Fill

    Type the missing term. Harder than recognising it in a list.

Our Mission

Sokrat Study is an educational platform developed by Leon Kreso, with a simple but powerful goal: to make learning easier, more accessible, and more effective for students everywhere.

What We Do

The platform helps learners prepare for exams by sharing high-quality study materials, summaries, and scripts across different subjects.
